DIN 912 M4 knurled round screw cap — millimetres

Revision: 15 mm cooled target round knob, 30 axial knurl grooves,
user-requested 1.6% uniform linear PLA shrinkage compensation.
Rib-to-head overlap: nominal 0.12 mm diametral, within the requested 0.10-0.15 mm range.

Files: M4_DIN912_thumb_cap.step is the main deliverable; STL is optional.
M4_fit_test.step/STL is a smaller cap with the same head pocket and peg.

SCREW AND INSTALLATION
Standard DIN 912 M4: nominal head diameter 7 mm, height 4 mm, hex socket 3 mm AF.
Align the hex peg and push the cap axially onto the exposed head.
The cap surrounds the upper 3.6 mm of the head, leaving nominally 0.4 mm
clearance above the mounting surface at the rim when seated.
Three ribs retain the cap on the head OD; the hex peg transmits torque.

COOLED TARGET DIMENSIONS
Maximum diameter 15 mm; height 7.6 mm; rounded exterior edges R0.5 mm.
30 axial grooves: circular cutter diameter 0.9 mm, centered at radius 7.45 mm;
radial groove depth approximately 0.5 mm. Grooves print vertically without supports.
Head pocket diameter 7.18 mm, depth 3.6 mm; entry flare diameter 7.98 mm.
Effective diameter at retention ribs 6.88 mm; nominal interference
0.12 mm diametrally (0.06 mm radially) on a 7.00 mm head.
The fit-test cap uses the same 6.88 mm cooled target rib diameter.
Hex peg AF 2.94 mm; insertion depth 1.8 mm; final 0.3 mm tapers to AF 2.45 mm.

SHRINKAGE COMPENSATION
Uniform X/Y/Z factor 1/(1-0.016) = 1.01626016. Exported dimensions:
Maximum diameter 15.244 mm; height 7.724 mm.
Head pocket diameter 7.297 mm; depth 3.659 mm; entry diameter 8.110 mm.
Effective rib diameter 6.992 mm; hex peg AF 2.988 mm; insertion 1.829 mm.
All fit dimensions are compensated along with the body.
The 1.6% figure is user-specified and not validated by a physical print.
If actual shrinkage is lower, the cap may fit loosely.
Do not apply a second shrinkage compensation in the slicer or filament profile.
Printer small-hole bias and screw manufacturing tolerances also affect fit.

BAMBU H2D / PLA BASIC
0.4 mm nozzle; calibrated PLA Basic profile; 0.16 mm layers; 4 walls;
5 top/bottom layers; 30–40% infill. Flat exterior face down at Z=0,
pocket and peg upward. No supports. Print at 100% scale.
Normal elephant-foot compensation; start without added XY/hole compensation.
Print the fit-test cap first, cool fully, align hex, and press on by hand.
If tight, lightly dress only the retention ribs. Do not force a misaligned peg.
If loose, tune the rib effective diameter in design.py in small steps;
do not change whole-part scale to adjust retention.

For light finger adjustment. No tightening torque rating is claimed.
PLA can strip, creep or soften at elevated temperatures.
Not physically tested; use specified tooling for joints requiring controlled preload.
