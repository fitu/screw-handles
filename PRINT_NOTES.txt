DIN 912 M4 knurled screw cap — millimetres

Revision: 15 mm cooled target knob, 30 straight knurl grooves,
1.6% linear PLA shrinkage compensation, ribs sized for minimum head diameter.

VERIFIED HEAD LIMITS
Smooth head diameter: 6.78 to 7.00 mm.
Knurled screw head maximum diameter: 7.22 mm; minimum remains 6.78 mm.
Head height: 3.82 to 4.00 mm; nominal hex socket 3 mm AF.
Sources:
https://eshop.boellhoff.de/out/media/pdf/DIN_912_Stahl_10.9___en.pdf
https://www.hjfasteners.com/userfiles/Fl202205302235287842.pdf

RIB TARGET AND FINISHED ACCEPTANCE
Cooled effective rib diameter target: 6.74 mm.
Finished acceptance maximum: 6.76 mm, allowing +0.02 mm process variation.
For a 6.78 mm minimum head: target interference 0.04 mm diametral;
accepted finished geometry gives at least 0.02 mm diametral interference.

Head diameter | Target interference | Interference at acceptance maximum
6.78 mm       | 0.04 mm diametral  | 0.02 mm diametral
7.00 mm       | 0.26 mm diametral  | 0.24 mm diametral
7.22 mm       | 0.48 mm diametral  | 0.46 mm diametral
Radial interference is half these values.

The 0.02 mm minimum requires verifying the cooled effective opening is at
most 6.76 mm. It is not guaranteed by an unmeasured print. Shrinkage variation
and printing error must remain within the +0.02 mm opening allowance.
Larger heads require more rib displacement. Insertion force and durability
have not been measured. The fit-test cap has the same pocket, ribs and peg.

COOLED GEOMETRY
Knob maximum diameter 15 mm; height 7.6 mm; exterior edge radius 0.5 mm.
30 axial grip grooves: 0.9 mm cutter diameter, center radius 7.45 mm.
Head pocket diameter 7.40 mm, depth 3.6 mm; entry flare diameter 8.20 mm.
The pocket clears the 7.22 mm maximum knurled screw head away from the ribs.
Three ribs at 120 degrees; rounded radius 0.38 mm; 2.8 mm constant section,
then individual 0.4 mm conical ramps tapering toward the pocket entrance.
Hex peg AF 2.94 mm; insertion 1.8 mm; final 0.3 mm tapers to AF 2.45 mm.
The rim surrounds the upper 3.6 mm of the head, leaving at least 0.22 mm
nominal mounting-surface clearance for a 3.82 mm minimum-height head.

PRINT / INSTALL
Bambu H2D; PLA Basic; calibrated profile; 0.4 mm nozzle; 0.16 mm layers;
4 walls; 5 top/bottom layers; 30–40% infill. Flat exterior face on the bed,
pocket and peg upward; no supports. Print at 100% scale.
Print the fit-test cap first and let it cool fully. Verify effective rib
opening and fit. Align hex before pressing on by hand; avoid forcing a
misaligned peg. If fit needs changing, adjust the rib parameter rather than
whole-part scale, and recheck the minimum-head interference requirement.

For light finger adjustment. No rated insertion force, durability or
tightening torque. Fit has not been physically validated. PLA can strip,
creep or soften at elevated temperatures.

Validation: STEP exports are checked as single valid solids and reimported.
