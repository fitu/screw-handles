DIN 912 M4 press-on wing cap — millimetres

Files: M4_DIN912_thumb_cap.step is the main deliverable. STL is optional.
M4_fit_test.step/STL is a smaller test cap with identical socket and retention geometry.

Assumed screw: standard DIN 912 M4, nominal head diameter 7 mm, height 4 mm,
hex socket 3 mm AF. Not for button heads, low heads or countersunk screws.
Slides axially over the head from its exposed face; align the peg with the socket.

COOLED TARGET DIMENSIONS (prior to compensation)
Wing envelope: 28 x 12 x 7.6 mm, rounded edges R0.5.
Head pocket: diameter 7.18 mm, depth 3.6 mm, entry flare to diameter 7.98.
Three rounded ribs at 120 degrees: minimum effective diameter 6.92 mm.
Nominal radial interference at ribs: 0.04 mm on a 7.00 mm head.
Pocket surrounds only the upper 3.6 mm of the 4 mm head so the rim clears
its mounting surface by nominally 0.4 mm when seated.
Hex peg: 2.94 mm AF, protrudes 1.8 mm from roof, last 0.3 mm tapered
to 2.45 mm AF. Clearance fit transmits torque; retention is on the head OD.

COMPENSATION
All geometry is scaled by 1/0.998 for an assumed 0.2% linear PLA shrinkage.
Exported nominal envelope: 28.056 x 12.024 x 7.615 mm (CAD bounding tolerances
may add a few microns). Rib effective diameter in CAD 6.934 mm;
pocket 7.194 mm; peg 2.946 mm AF.
This is a starting allowance, not a measured calibration for your spool/H2D.
Small-hole extrusion bias and screw manufacturing tolerance can exceed the
shrinkage adjustment. Do not add another global shrink scale in the slicer.

BAMBU H2D / PLA BASIC
Use a 0.4 mm nozzle, calibrated PLA Basic profile, 0.16 mm layers, 4 walls,
5 top/bottom layers, 30-40% infill. Print the flat exterior face at Z=0 on the
bed, pocket and peg pointing up. No supports. Use normal elephant-foot
compensation; avoid XY/hole compensation initially because it changes the fit.
Print the fit-test cap first and let it cool fully. Align the hex before
pressing by hand. If too tight, remove and lightly dress only the ribs;
do not force the PLA hex into a misaligned socket. If loose, adjust the rib
parameter in the source by 0.04-0.08 mm in effective diameter, not whole-part scale.
The CAD-generation script is supplied for dimensional edits (requires CadQuery).

For light finger adjustment. PLA socket drives can strip under high torque;
no tightening torque rating is claimed. Not a substitute for a torque wrench
where the M4 joint requires a specified preload. Retention and torque capacity
have not been physically tested; PLA can creep or soften in hot conditions.

CAD validation: each STEP reimported successfully as one valid solid.
