DIN 912 M4 knurled cap — nominal CAD dimensions, mm

No shrinkage compensation. No global scaling. This revision supersedes
all earlier compensated dimensions and rib acceptance limits.

OD 20.00 at knurl lands; head bore ID 7.50 away from ribs.
Three ribs at 120 degrees; bottom effective ID 6.65 at the pocket floor.
Rib draft 3 degrees towards a wider opening, over a 3.00 mm rib height.
Effective ID at rib free ends: 6.96445.
Bottom rib radius 0.38; free-end radius 0.22278, fixed radial centre 3.705.
Each rib is a cone frustum; inner contact face has the requested 3 degree draft.

Overall height 7.60. Pocket floor Z=4.00. Pocket depth 3.60.
Ribs end at Z=7.00. Chamfer starts Z=7.20: 0.20 mm axial clear space.
Clear space has full bore ID 7.50. Mouth chamfer 0.40 x 45 degrees;
mouth ID 8.30 at Z=7.60. Ribs do not enter the chamfer or clear band.

Hex driving section AF 3.02; protrusion from pocket floor 1.80.
Final 0.30 tapers to AF 2.53 for entry. All dimensions are unscaled.
40 straight knurl grooves, 0.90 cutter diameter at radius 9.95;
outer edge rounding R0.50.
